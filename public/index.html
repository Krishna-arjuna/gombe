THE GOMBE WAY

GOOD TO KNOW

Thoughtful things,
thoughtful answers.

Questions about a piece, delivery or gifting? We’re here to help.

How should I choose a toy for a child?+

Check the recommended age on each product and supervise play where appropriate. If you need more product or safety details, contact us before ordering.

How are delivery and shipping fees calculated?+

Shipping fees are shown in your order total. Delivery timing can vary by destination; contact us before ordering if you need an estimate.

Can I send an order as a gift?+

Yes. Choose gift options at checkout to include gift wrapping and a personal note. Prices are not included on the gift note.

What if I need to return or exchange an item?+

Eligibility can vary by item and delivery location. Please contact our team with your order reference so we can help with the available options.

How do I care for hand-turned wooden toys?+

Wipe gently with a soft, dry cloth and keep away from prolonged moisture. Contact us for care guidance for a specific piece.
