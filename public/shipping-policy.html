Shipping & Returns

Every Gombe creation begins in Channapatna, India. Here's how we deliver our handcrafted pieces to you and how we help when an order arrives damaged or defective.

Orders of ₹1,000 or more FREE Free national shipping

Returns & Replacements

We want every Gombe creation to reach you in good condition. If your product arrives damaged or defective, please contact Gombe within 7 days from the date of delivery.

  • Returns and replacements are limited to damaged or defective products.
  • Gombe arranges pickup for eligible products.
  • There is no return pickup charge for eligible damaged or defective products.

This policy does not provide general returns for change of mind.
